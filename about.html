Story

From posters to products.

My interest in design and solving problems led me into product and creative design. It is a continuous path, and curiosity keeps me learning how people interact with products and services.

As a Product Designer at ToNote Technologies I redesigned critical systems, including the company’s Electronic Register and its website, and created custom workflows for B2B and B2G clients like Carbon and the SEC. I also collaborated on the Plenty Waka bus ride-hailing redesign, leading the move to smoother onboarding and better interface components.

As a creative designer I have built brands and content for ToNote and Future Software Resources, and digital campaigns for UPDC Custodian and Always Me by Anne Li. I work best where I can push creative boundaries and keep the end user at the centre of every decision.

Process

How every project moves.

  1. I

    Understand

    Talk to the people involved and audit what exists, to find the real problem.

  2. II

    Contextualise

    Map users, constraints and the business goal behind the brief.

  3. III

    Explore

    Sketch wide, then narrow to the strongest direction with stakeholders.

  4. IV

    Validate

    Prototype and test before polish, so decisions rest on evidence.

  5. V

    Build

    Hand off precise specs and stay close through launch.
