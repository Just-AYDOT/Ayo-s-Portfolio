Role

Overview

Two jobs at once: steward the brand, and ship the product.

At ToNote Technologies my work is rooted in advancing the ToNote brand. I manage brand assets across the company’s social platforms and create the materials ToNote takes to events.

Alongside the brand, I play a central role in product. I led the redesign of the company website to improve the experience and bring it in line with the brand, and I designed custom B2B workflows for key clients including Carbon and the SEC.

Social media

A steady voice on social media.

Product

A website and workflows built for trust.

The website redesign made it clearer how to sign and notarise a document online, from the first visit to a completed, sealed file. For enterprise clients I designed custom workflows and admin tools around how their teams actually approve documents.

Merch ideas that carry ToNote into everyday use: bottles, keyrings and flash drives.
