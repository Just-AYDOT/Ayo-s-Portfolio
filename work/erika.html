Erika Beach House : a timeless wordmark with indigenous roots.

Role

Brand designer

Tools

Figma, Adobe Creative Suite

Deliverables

Wordmark, colourways, merchandise

The brief

Timeless, and right for today.

Erika is a beach house in Ikare. It needed a logo that feels timeless and still relevant to the present day.

The strategy was a simple, functional mark that works in formal presentation material and on apparel that supporters and clients would be proud to wear.

The logo

A wordmark with an African tilt.

The mark is a wordmark built from the name ERIKA. It leans towards African culture, so Erika reads as an indigenous brand and not only as a beach house.

The letters are evenly spaced, with the spacing stepped up by adding the initial size to the current value.

Colourways

Six colourways, one mark.

The wordmark works across a set of earthy colourways and holds up on merchandise like tote bags.

Alternatives

Directions explored on the way.

Other directions set the wordmark on blocks of colour, tried it on a cap, and paired it with a palm motif.
