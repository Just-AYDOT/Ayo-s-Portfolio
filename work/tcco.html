The Community Company : connecting SMB communities.

Role

Product designer

Tools

Figma

Client

The Community Company (TCCo.)

§1

Overview

Thriving communities inspire growth.

TCCo. connects small and medium businesses through integrated products, services and experiences. Its mission is to transform how SMB ventures operate using ecosystem networks and digital infrastructure, and to reach over 3,000 businesses in three years.

The website is the front door: it introduces the community, its spaces and brands, a directory of member businesses, and a simple way to join.

§2

Impact

Proof up front.

The homepage leads with a bold, editorial headline, then puts the numbers straight after: over 1 million website visitors, 12,000+ connections and 50+ SMBs, followed by the mission.

§3

Spaces & brands

Places to belong.

The Engage page brings the community’s brands together, from the CaFFa House community hub and Culture Night Popup to The Culture Mail newsletter, Venture Stack and Traydah.

§4

Join

Community, excellence, partnership, integrity, authenticity.

The values run through photography from real community events, and every page ends with one call to action: join The Community Collective.
