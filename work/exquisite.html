The Exquisite Group : a regal identity for keeping languages alive.

Role

Brand designer

Tools

Figma, Adobe Creative Suite

Deliverables

Logo, sub-brands, palette, mockups

Overview

Bridging the gap between generations.

Many immigrant children in Canada are slowly losing the ability to talk with their grandparents, and their native languages are fading with them. The Exquisite Group works on both integration into Canada and the preservation of native cultures.

The logo

EQ, for Exquisite.

The logo joins the letters E and Q into an acronym for the name. The elements around it suggest excellence and give the brand a regal feel, and every element is evenly spaced.

The mark locks up in several ways depending on use: a primary logo, sub-brands for The Literary Society and The Exquisite Club, and a standalone icon.

Colour

A restrained palette that pairs well.

The colours reflect the brand’s focus on excellence. Shades and tints work together, and the logo was tested on each background. The palette stays restrained so it holds across every branded asset.

In use

From business cards to lanyards.

Mockups put the identity on business cards, a cap and a lanyard.
