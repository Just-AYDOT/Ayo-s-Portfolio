The Brane Project : a mark for growth, innovation and wealth.

Role

Brand designer

Tools

Figma, Adobe Creative Suite

Deliverables

Logo options, rationale, mockups

Objective

Growth, innovation and wealth, at first glance.

Brane needed a logo that conveys financial growth, innovation and wealth creation the moment you see it.

Design

Two routes, both built on the B.

I explored marks that lean into the key words in the objective. Each one starts from B, the first letter of the name, and several of them went on to shape the wider visual identity.

Option 1 pairs the B with a raised top cap for peak, height and progression, which reads as growth. Option 2 pairs it with a precious stone for wealth and value, which the brand is built on.

In use

How the mark holds up in the real world.

Mockups put the logo on a hanging sign and a glass facade, to check that it translates well across formats and sizes.
