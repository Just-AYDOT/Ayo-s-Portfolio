Electronic Notary Register: Nigeria’s official record of notaries public.

Role

Product designer

Tools

Figma

Client

Supreme Court of Nigeria

§1

Overview

One place to find, verify and work with a notary.

The Electronic Notary Register is the Supreme Court’s official digital directory of every appointed and enrolled Notary Public in Nigeria. It protects the public from unauthorised and fraudulent notarial practice.

Anyone can find a certified notary, book a session or verify a document. Notaries use the same platform to join the register, digitise their seal and stamp, and submit notarised documents to the Supreme Court repository.

§2

Find a notary

Find a certified notary in your state.

Search by state to browse certified notaries, each with a location, rating and the kinds of notarisation they offer. Or skip the search and book the first available notary.

§3

Verification

Verification in a few steps.

Two short forms do the checking: enter a Document ID to confirm a notarised document, or a Supreme Court number to confirm a notary. Results come straight from the official registry.

§4

For notaries

A home for the notary community.

Notaries sign in to register, digitise their seal and stamp, request a physical seal and submit notarised documents. The Notary League ranks notaries on performance and client service, and resets every year.

§5

Online notarisation

Notarise a document in five steps.

Remote online notarisation is laid out as five steps: select a notary, add your details and document, pay, verify your identity and join the session, then receive the notarised document.
