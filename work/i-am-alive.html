I Am Alive: proof of life for pensioners, without the queue.

Role

Product designer

Tools

Figma

Client

ToNote, for PFAs

§1

Overview

Proving you’re alive shouldn’t put a pension at risk.

Retirees must regularly confirm they are alive to keep receiving their pension. I Am Alive (Pulse, by ToNote) lets them do it from a text message link, with no app and no account.

I designed all three products: the retiree’s phone flow, the dashboard Pension Fund Administrators use to monitor and decide checks, and a workspace for verification agents on web and mobile. About 150 screens in all.

Note

Many of these projects are under NDA. Ask me for a private walkthrough.

§2

Retiree flow

Three steps, four ways to verify.

Retirees choose how first: a one-minute liveness check on the phone, a video call, an office visit or a home visit. All are free and give the same certificate. Every screen offers bigger text, read aloud and help, and a reminder that we never ask for an ATM PIN.

§3

Certificate

A Certificate of Life anyone can check.

Once verified, the retiree gets a Certificate of Life by text, with a QR code. Scanning it opens a public page that confirms the certificate is real, without showing the retiree’s photo, phone or address.

§4

PFA dashboard

A dashboard built around the 5-day rule.

PenCom requires a failed check to be decided within 5 working days. The dashboard opens on a Do today list sorted by that clock, and a side-by-side review lets staff approve, ask for another check, or report suspected fraud.

§5

Agent workspace

Agents who pick up the work themselves.

Agents sign themselves up and pick up home visits and video calls by fastest fingers: the first to accept gets it. At the door they say a 4-digit code, then take a photo to complete the check.
