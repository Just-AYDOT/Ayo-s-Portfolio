Cobalt: a premium mark for safe, fair currency exchange.

Role

Brand designer

Tools

Figma, Adobe Illustrator

Deliverables

Logo, icon, pattern, mockups

The brief

A new day for cross-border payments.

Cobalt is redefining how people exchange foreign currency at home and abroad, while giving them better value for their time and money.

The brand set out to disrupt slow processes, irregular rates and inefficient FX operations, so that people experience Cobalt for what it is: a safe exchange system.

Design

Two hands, one exchange.

The identity is premium and welcoming to anyone who wants a better way to exchange currency at home and abroad. The mark combines two human hands, one reversed, carrying out an exchange.

Brand pattern

A pattern built from the mark.

The icon repeats into a brand pattern that carries the identity across banners, stationery, tote bags and street signage, signed off with the line “easy does it”.
