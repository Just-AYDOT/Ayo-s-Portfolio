Gender Centre of Excellence: an identity where everyone stands together.

Role

Brand designer

Tools

Figma, Adobe Illustrator

Deliverables

Logo, icon, campaign assets

The brief

Support for the people banks leave out.

The Gender Centre of Excellence (GCE) is a strategic resource centre supporting Nigeria’s financial-inclusion ecosystem. It helps organisations design, implement and sustain gender-responsive policies, products and services for unbanked and underbanked people, particularly low-income women.

GCE needed a brand identity for its campaign and several of its projects.

Design

Inclusive from the first sketch.

The mark had to lean into what GCE represents as a group, and into its vision of empowerment for both men and women. Inclusivity guided every step, from ideation and concepts through to decisions with stakeholders.

Explorations

What stakeholders asked for.

  1. 1A logo that is distinct and versatile.
  2. 2The acronym “GCE” inside the mark.
  3. 3An identity that is gender-neutral, not tilted to women alone.

My job was to balance craft with that feedback. The final logo combines two of the early directions.

Final result

People holding hands around a coin.

The full mark shows men and women holding hands in unison, standing for vibrance, balance, women standing tall, community and inclusion. The coin at its centre speaks to finance and wealth.
