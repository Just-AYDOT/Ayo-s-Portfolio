EPTCMS: the Supreme Court’s case file for election appeals.

Role

Product and brand designer

Tools

Figma

Client

Supreme Court of Nigeria

§1

Overview

Giving election appeals the authority of the court.

EPTCMS is the Election Petition Tribunal Case Management System for the Supreme Court of Nigeria. Lawyers and litigants file appeals, the registry reviews and records them, justices hear them and bailiffs serve process, all in one system.

I designed the interface, the design system behind it and the logo. Every screen, notice and printout had to feel calm, exact and formal, with national green used with restraint.

Note

Many of these projects are under NDA. Ask me for a private walkthrough.

§2

Users

Eight user groups, one appeal page.

Lawyers, litigants in person, registrars, the Head of Litigation, justices, bailiffs, the Chief Registrar and system administrators all work from the same appeal page; only their actions change. Bailiffs and litigants use phones, so their screens are designed phone-first.

§3

Key flows

From filing to judgment.

Each dashboard opens with a Start here card and the next task, so every role knows what to do first. A statutory clock and an activity timeline sit beside every appeal, because deadlines decide election cases.

§4

Design system

A design system with the court’s voice.

Source Serif 4 is the court’s voice, IBM Plex Sans runs the interface and IBM Plex Mono sets appeal numbers. Green marks the one primary action, and every status is spelled out in words, never colour alone.

§5

Identity

A logo that reads as a court, not an election.

I explored three directions for the mark: the weighed ballot, the sealed ballot and a tribunal monogram. The recommended direction puts the scales of justice at the centre, weighing a ballot paper.
